When you get paid

  • Once a month, by PayPal, Wise or bank transfer.
  • Each payment is counted 30 days after the player pays, so refunds can come back first.
  • Minimum payout $50. Smaller amounts roll over to the next month.
  • Your private earnings page shows sign-ups, sales and what you're owed at any time. It shows totals only, never who the players are.

The rules

  • Your player stays yours. The first creator code a player uses is the one that counts, and it can't be swapped for another creator's code later.
  • The code must come before the first purchase. Players who already paid before entering a code don't count.
  • 12 months per player. You earn on every payment a player makes in the 12 months after they used your code: new subscriptions, renewals, and re-subscribing after cancelling. After 12 months, that player no longer earns you a share. Using your link again does not restart the 12 months.
  • Refunds. If Apple refunds a payment, your share of it is taken back from your next payout.
  • No self-referral. Your own account can't use your code. Fake sign-ups, paid ads on our brand name and misleading claims end the partnership and forfeit unpaid earnings.
  • Net means the price after Apple's fee, sales tax or VAT, and refunds.
  • We may change these terms with 30 days' notice. Payments already earned are always paid.
