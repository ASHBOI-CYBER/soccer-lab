Soccer Lab Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

Soccer Lab is a training app for soccer players. This page explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and how to delete it. Soccer Lab is published on the App Store by [Publisher name], Australia ("we").

The short version

  • Most of what you do in Soccer Lab stays on your phone: your training log, film reviews and video clips, food log and the routes of your runs.
  • If you sign in with Apple, we keep a small account so teams, chat and backups work.
  • If you use the AI Coach, your messages and a short training summary are sent to Anthropic to write the answer. You're asked first.
  • We don't sell your information, and we don't show ads.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time: Profile, Settings, Delete account.

What stays on your phone

Your answers from setting up the app, your training sessions and streak, drills you tick off, film reviews and any clips you attach, your food and water log, your body details for fuel targets, and your run routes are stored only on your device. Clips you pick from Photos are copied into the app's own storage and are never uploaded.

Your contacts are only read on your phone when you choose to invite a friend, and are never uploaded. Location for runs is only used while a run is recording, and the route stays on your phone.

Anthropic processes AI Coach requests to produce the answer and, under its commercial terms, does not use them to train its models. The AI Coach can be wrong and is not medical advice.

Payments

Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your card details. Manage or cancel a subscription in your iPhone's Settings, under your name, Subscriptions.

How long we keep information

Account, team and backup data are kept until you delete your account. AI Coach message counts are kept for 14 days to apply the daily limit; we don't store the conversation on our server. Purchase records are kept as long as needed for accounting and tax.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Profile, Settings, Delete account. This removes your account, profile, team memberships, posts and backup from our server straight away. Data on your phone is removed when you delete the app. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Young players

Soccer Lab is made for players aged 13 and over. If you are under the age where your country requires a parent's permission to use online services, please use Soccer Lab with a parent or guardian's permission. If you believe a child has given us information without that permission, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Australian privacy law and the GDPR, you can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to how it is used. Contact us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
